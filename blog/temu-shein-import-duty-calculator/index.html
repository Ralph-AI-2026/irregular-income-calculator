What Will Your Temu or Shein Order Actually Cost? (Import Duty Calculator for Canada and the US)

You fill a Temu cart at $50. That's the price you see. Then a notice card shows up at your door and suddenly you owe another $26 before the package is yours. The Import Duty Calculator shows you that number before you hit buy, not after.

Shopping on Temu, Shein, or AliExpress has a math problem most people discover at the door. The checkout price is real, but it is not the price you actually pay. Customs duty, sales tax, and a carrier handling fee stack on top, and none of them show up at checkout until it is too late.

In 2026, this problem got worse for shoppers in both Canada and the US. Canada's duty-free threshold for China-direct orders is CAD $20, meaning virtually every order gets taxed. The US eliminated its $800 de minimis entirely, making every parcel from China dutiable regardless of value. The gap between "what you paid" and "what it costs to receive it" is wider than it has ever been.

Temu Customs Fees in Canada: What You Are Actually Paying

Does Temu charge duty to Canadian buyers?

Temu does not collect customs duty on your behalf at checkout on most orders. The duty, sales tax, and carrier handling fee are assessed when the parcel arrives in Canada and are collected by Canada Post or the courier before they release the package to you. On some recent Temu orders, a line labeled "import fees" or "estimated import charges" has appeared at checkout. That is Temu collecting an estimate upfront. Whether the amount matches what CBSA actually assesses varies. If Temu undercollects, CBSA will assess the difference on arrival.

See the dedicated section below on "Does Temu collect duty for you at checkout?" for a plain-language breakdown of what that checkout line means.

What is the de minimis threshold in Canada?

The general de minimis threshold for goods shipped by mail or courier directly from China is CAD $20. Any order over $20 is subject to full import duty plus provincial sales tax at the border (CBSA Memo D2-3-6; CBSA D8-2-16, as of June 2026). There is no meaningful buffer for Temu, Shein, or AliExpress orders.

This trips people up because it is often confused with Canada's CUSMA thresholds. Under CUSMA (the successor to NAFTA), courier shipments from the US or Mexico receive a CAD $40 tax-free threshold and a CAD $150 duty-free threshold. Those rules apply only when goods ship from within the US or Mexico and the goods qualify as originating there. Temu, Shein, and AliExpress ship from China directly. The CUSMA thresholds do not apply. The CAD $20 line is the one that counts.

Practical result: a $22 clothing order from Temu to Ontario is not a small cheap purchase. It is $22.00 + $3.96 duty (18%, CBSA Tariff Ch. 61/62) + $3.37 HST at 13% on the duty-inclusive value of $25.96 + $9.95 Canada Post handling fee = $39.28 total for a $22 item. Nearly double the checkout price.

Why did you get a Canada Post customs handling fee?

Canada Post charges a flat $9.95 per dutiable item to process your parcel through customs on your behalf (Canada Post published fee schedule, as of June 2026). The fee applies to every parcel over the CAD $20 threshold, regardless of order size. It is separate from the duty and separate from the tax. It is not a government charge. It is Canada Post's fee for the administrative work of collecting duty and tax on CBSA's behalf.

On a small order where duty is only $3 or $4, the $9.95 handling fee is often the biggest single line on the bill. The Import Duty Calculator shows it as its own line so you can see exactly what you are paying and why.

FedEx, DHL, and UPS brokerage fees: the biggest surprise

If your Temu or Shein order ships by private courier rather than Canada Post mail, the brokerage fee is larger and more variable than the $9.95 Canada Post flat fee. See the full carrier comparison table in the section below. The short version: UPS Standard charges $19.45 for orders between $40 and $100, DHL Express charges 2.5% of the combined duty and tax with a minimum of $17.50, and FedEx Ground charges roughly $25.50 for low-value informal entries (SECONDARY source, based on broker data as Canada Customs Tariff primary is not publicly available for FedEx Ground). FedEx Express and UPS Express bundle clearance into the shipping rate and charge nothing extra.

The shipping method your order uses matters as much as the product category. A $40 clothing order can owe $9.95 in Canada Post handling or $19.45 in UPS brokerage depending entirely on how it ships. The calculator lets you toggle this.

Shein and AliExpress Customs Fees in Canada

Does Shein collect taxes at checkout in Canada?

Shein began collecting GST/HST on Canadian orders in 2023 following Canada's digital services tax rules that required large foreign platforms to register and remit. This means Shein collects the federal and provincial tax at checkout for most Canadian orders. However, Shein's checkout collection does not eliminate the import duty on the goods themselves, and it does not cover the carrier handling fee. If CBSA assesses duty on arrival (which it will, for any clothing order over CAD $20), and the carrier has not been pre-paid, you will still receive a notice card.

In practice, a Shein clothing order to Canada may have tax already collected at checkout but still attract a $9.95 Canada Post handling fee on arrival. The Import Duty Calculator lets you see all the components separately so you can assess what Shein has or has not covered.

AliExpress orders to Canada: when do you pay duty?

AliExpress orders to Canada follow the same rules as Temu. Goods shipped directly from China are subject to the CAD $20 general de minimis. Every order over $20 owes full duty plus provincial sales tax. The duty rate depends on the product category (clothing 18%, electronics 0%, cosmetics 6.5%, per Canada Customs Tariff 2025, CBSA). The carrier handling fee applies on top regardless of whether AliExpress has collected anything at checkout. AliExpress does not universally collect Canadian taxes at checkout the way Shein has begun to do, so duty-on-arrival is the more common experience for AliExpress buyers.

The US Side: Temu Import Charges After the De Minimis Change

What does "import charges" mean on my Temu order?

Since the US eliminated the $800 de minimis exemption for all countries in 2026, Temu now shows an "import charges" line at checkout on US orders. This is Temu's estimate of the duty stack your parcel will owe at the border: the MFN base rate (around 16% for clothing, 0% for electronics under the WTO ITA), a 25% Section 301 China-specific tariff (fully in force, unaffected by recent court rulings, CBP/USITC as of June 2026), and a 10% Section 122 global surcharge (currently being collected by CBP but facing active litigation and a statutory expiry around July 24, 2026 unless Congress acts).

The "import charges" line is an estimate. CBP classifies goods independently, and the actual assessed amount may differ from Temu's upfront estimate. The Import Duty Calculator shows the full US tariff stack as a dated estimate with a clear note about the Section 122 volatility.

Does Shein include tariffs in the price?

Shein began rolling out duty-inclusive pricing for US customers after the de minimis change. On some product pages and at checkout, Shein absorbs or pre-collects the estimated duty so the price you see reflects the landed cost. This does not mean you owe nothing to CBP. It means Shein is paying the duty on your behalf and has built the cost into the price. The actual duty rate (base MFN + Section 301 25% + Section 122 10%) does not change. What changes is who pays it and when.

If Shein's included estimate is lower than what CBP actually assesses, the difference will be collected on arrival. If Shein's price is higher than the duty owed, Shein keeps the overage.

Does Temu Collect Duty for You at Checkout?

On some Temu orders, you will see a line at checkout labeled "import fees," "estimated import charges," or similar. Temu began adding this after the US eliminated its $800 de minimis in 2026, and it has appeared on some Canadian orders as well. Here is what that line actually means:

Temu is collecting an estimate of the duty it expects CBSA or CBP to assess when the parcel arrives. By collecting it upfront, Temu pre-pays the duty on your behalf so you do not receive a notice card at the door. This is convenient, and it removes the $9.95 Canada Post handling fee surprise because the carrier will have been pre-paid before delivery.

However, it is an estimate. Temu's estimate is based on the product category and the destination country. If CBSA assesses a higher duty rate than Temu estimated, the carrier may still collect the difference from you on arrival. If Temu overcollected, you generally do not receive a refund automatically. The collection is Temu's estimate of what the rules require, not a guarantee that nothing will be assessed at the door.

On orders where Temu has not added an import fee line at checkout, the duty, tax, and carrier handling fee will be collected on arrival in the traditional way.

Will Temu and Shein Prices Go Up in Canada Like They Did in the US?

The dramatic US price increases that Temu and Shein announced in early 2026 were driven by one specific change: the US eliminated its $800 de minimis exemption, making every China-direct parcel dutiable. With a 25% Section 301 tariff plus a 10% Section 122 surcharge on top of the MFN base rate, a $50 clothing order's US duty stack alone exceeds $25.

Canada's situation is different in a key way. Canada did not change its de minimis threshold in 2026. The CAD $20 threshold for China-direct goods has been in place for years. Canadian shoppers were already paying duty on virtually every Temu, Shein, and AliExpress order before 2026. Canada also does not have an equivalent to the US Section 301 China tariff or the Section 122 global surcharge. Canada's MFN duty rate for clothing is 18%, which is significant, but it does not stack with additional China-specific punitive tariffs the way the US stack does.

The bigger variable for Canadian buyers is not tariff policy but shipping method. If Temu and Shein shift more shipments to courier rather than Canada Post mail to speed up delivery, shoppers could face courier brokerage fees of $19.45 to $25.50 instead of the $9.95 Canada Post flat fee. That shift would raise the effective landed cost without any change in duty rates. Watch your shipping method, not the headlines about US tariff policy.
