Side Hustle True Cost Calculator -- How Much Are You Really Making?

The app says $25/hr. After taxes, fees, gas, and wear on your car, the real number is a lot less. Find out what you actually take home.

Quick answer: Most gig workers earn 40-60% less than what the app shows them. A DoorDash driver who thinks they make $25/hr often takes home $10-$14/hr after self-employment tax, gas, vehicle depreciation, and platform fees.
