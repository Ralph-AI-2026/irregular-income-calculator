Cancellation Guide

How to Cancel Your Zombie Subscriptions

Step-by-step instructions for the most common services. Most take under 2 minutes.

Subscription Audit

Find Subscriptions You Forgot About

Work through this checklist to uncover every recurring charge. Most people find 3-5 forgotten subscriptions in under 30 minutes.

Search your email inbox for receipt keywords
Open Gmail, Outlook, or your email app and search for these terms one at a time:
receipt subscription renewal charge billing payment confirmation invoice
Review 3 months of bank/credit card statements
Log in to your bank and go through the last 3 months line by line. Flag any charge you don't immediately recognize. Pay special attention to amounts under $20 -- those are the ones that hide in plain sight.
Check iPhone App Store subscriptions
Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions. You'll see every active and expired subscription billed through Apple. Cancel anything you don't use.
Check Google Play subscriptions
Open the Google Play Store app > Menu > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions. Review everything listed and cancel what you no longer need.
Check PayPal recurring payments
Log in to PayPal > Settings > Payments > Manage Automatic Payments. Many forgotten subscriptions route through PayPal and don't show up on your credit card directly.
Check Amazon Subscribe & Save
Log in to Amazon > Accounts & Lists > Subscribe & Save. Review all active subscriptions and scheduled deliveries. Cancel anything you forgot was auto-shipping.
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

The average American spends about $91/month on subscriptions. Millennials spend the most at $112/month. Most people underestimate their spending by around 60%, thinking they spend about $40/month when the reality is more than double. This calculator eliminates guessing by letting you add each subscription individually.
Zombie subscriptions are services you pay for but rarely or never use. Research shows about 42% of all subscriptions fall into this category. They survive because cancellation is inconvenient and the monthly charge feels small enough to ignore. A subscription audit every 6 months is the best way to kill them.
Search your email for words like "receipt", "subscription", and "renewal". Then review 3 months of bank and credit card statements line by line. Also check your App Store, Google Play, and PayPal for recurring payments. Most people find 3-5 forgotten subscriptions in under 30 minutes using this method.
Most people save $50-150/month after a thorough subscription audit. That is $600-1,800 per year. If you invested the $91/month average waste at 7% annual returns, it would grow to over $15,000 in 10 years. Even cancelling 2-3 services you don't use can make a meaningful difference.
If you live in Canada and pay in Canadian dollars, toggle on CAD pricing. Canadian subscription costs are typically 20-30% higher due to exchange rates and regional pricing strategies. The calculator adjusts all pre-filled prices to approximate Canadian equivalents so your results are accurate.
